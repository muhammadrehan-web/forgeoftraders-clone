7-Day Reset Protection
If a qualifying drawdown limit is reached within the first 7 days of a challenge stage, the account is eligible for a one-time reset in accordance with the published program rules.
The account is restored to the higher of the two End-of-Day (EOD) values from the previous trading day: simulated Equity or simulated Balance. This allows the trader to retain as much progress as possible when the reset is applied.
                            

                        

                    

                

                    
                            

                    

                    

                                                

                        

                            
                        

                    


                    
                    

                        

                            

                                
                            


                            

                                
Enhanced Reward Share Add-on
This add-on increases your eligible performance reward share to 90%, based on simulated trading results and subject to the published program rules.
The enhanced reward share remains active for the duration of the account.
                            

                        

                    

                

                    
                            

                    

                    

                                                

                        

                            
                        

                    


                    
                    

                        

                            

                                
                            


                            

                                
Swing Trading Add-on
This add-on allows traders to hold positions open over the weekend and trade during restricted news windows.
It removes the standard weekend-holding and news-trading restrictions. All other trading and risk-management rules remain applicable.
